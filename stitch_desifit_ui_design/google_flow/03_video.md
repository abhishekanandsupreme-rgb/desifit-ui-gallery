# 03 — Video / Motion Prompts (Veo 3)

> **How to use:** Flow generates 4s / 6s / 8s / 10s clips with **native audio**
> (ambience + sound effects). Keep clips short and loopable — these become hero
> cinemagraphs, onboarding backgrounds, and transition plates. Always open with
> the ingredient reference, specify duration + aspect ratio, and describe motion
> explicitly (what moves, what stays still).
